# Gtan plan CEO review

## Prime directives

- Challenge scope first (nuclear scope challenge), then select mode.
- Modes: Expansion (10-star rethink), Selective Expansion (keep core, expand leverage points), Hold Scope, Reduction (cut to wedge).

## Checklist

- Retrospective check: what has been tried, what did learnings say.
- Landscape check: competitors, prior art.
- Taste calibration for expansion modes.
- Verdict per section: keep / amend / cut, with reason.

## Output

Amended plan with mode named, decisions surfaced for approval. Eng review runs last on the amended plan.
