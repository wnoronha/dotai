# Gtan plan devex review

## First principles

Seven DX characteristics, scored 0–10 with calibration. TTHW (time to hello world) benchmarked against competitors. Hall-of-fame reference for what great looks like.

## Modes

- **DX EXPANSION:** rethink the developer journey.
- **DX POLISH:** refine the planned surface.
- **DX TRIAGE:** cut scope to the friction that matters most.

## Checklist

- Personas named; journey traced step by step.
- Friction points enumerated with evidence.
- Error messages scored for the agents that read them (pairs with `cli-for-agents`).
- 20–45 forcing questions; keep one decision list across all phases.

## Output

Amended plan with DX scores, benchmarks, and required fixes. Eng review re-runs on the amended plan.
